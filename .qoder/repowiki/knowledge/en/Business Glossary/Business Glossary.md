### Podium Momentum
- Definition：The brand name of the agency whose portfolio site this repo builds; appears as the constant `BRAND` and in page titles/meta.
- Aliases：podiummomentum、podium-momentum

### Let's chat
- Definition：Primary CTA label used across the site (header button, case footers, etc.) that navigates to the contact form at `/contact-us`.
- Aliases：talk to us

### Showreel
- Definition：The branded looping video asset (`showreel.mp4`) embedded in the About section and hero area, with its own player controls and expanded modal viewer.
- Aliases：who we are video

### Selected work
- Definition：The portfolio/case-studies section listing projects such as JobPosted, Rezoomed, and LikeOn with sector, tags, stats, and timeline metadata.
- Aliases：work、projects
